Check my device

Help & playback settings

Short guides for your device. Set them once, in about two minutes, and you get the best picture with far fewer pauses.

Why these settings matter

When your device is set to Maximum / Original quality with Direct Play on, it plays the file exactly as it was made — no changes, no extra work in the middle. That gives you the sharpest picture and sound your TV can show.

If the quality is set low, the server has to rebuild every movie or show on the fly to fit your settings. That is called transcoding. It can make the picture softer, cause buffering, and it puts heavy work on the server for everyone. These quick settings protect the server and give you a better experience at the same time.

1. Quality = Maximum

Always choose Maximum, Original, or the highest number you see. Never leave it on Auto low or a small Mbps limit.

2. Direct Play = On

Turn on Direct Play and Direct Stream where you see them. This is the setting that avoids transcoding.

3. Use good Wi-Fi

Use the 5 GHz Wi-Fi network if you have one, keep the device close to the router, or plug in a network cable if you can.

Roku

Roku Stick, Roku box & Roku TV

Set quality & Direct Play

  1. Open the movies & series app and go to Settings (gear icon).
  2. Open Video. Set Local Quality and Remote Quality to Original / Maximum.
  3. Turn on Allow Direct Play and Allow Direct Stream.
  4. If you see Maximum allowed bitrate, set it to No limit.

If it buffers or the picture looks soft:

  • Check the two quality settings above again — an update can reset them.
  • Restart the Roku: Settings → System → Power → System restart.
  • Try subtitles off for a minute. Some subtitle types force transcoding; if it plays fine without them, tell us and we will help you pick a better subtitle option.

ONN 4K

ONN 4K & ONN 4K Pro streaming box

Set quality & Direct Play

  1. Open the movies & series app, go to your profile picture, then Settings.
  2. Open Video Quality. Set Home streaming and Internet streaming to Maximum.
  3. In Advanced (if shown), make sure Direct Play is On / Auto and not Disabled.
  4. On the ONN itself: Settings → Display & Sound → set resolution to 4K or Auto, so the box does not downscale.

If it buffers or transcodes:

  • Use the 5 GHz Wi-Fi network — the ONN box works much better on it for 4K.
  • Close other apps and restart the box once. Hold the power button on the remote and choose Restart.
  • If one movie keeps pausing but others play fine, note its name and message us — do not lower the quality for everything.

Amazon Fire TV Stick

Fire TV Stick, Fire TV Cube & Fire TV

Set quality & Direct Play

  1. Open the movies & series app and go to Settings → Video Quality.
  2. Set Home streaming and Internet streaming to Maximum.
  3. Turn Direct Play to On. Leave Direct Stream on as well.
  4. Under Fire TV Settings → Display & Sounds → Display, set Video Resolution to Auto (up to 4K).

If it buffers or transcodes:

  • Restart the Stick: Settings → My Fire TV → Restart. This clears most stutters.
  • Keep the Stick plugged into wall power, not a TV USB port — low power causes pauses.
  • Move the router closer or use 5 GHz Wi-Fi. If audio is set to a format your TV cannot play, that can also force transcoding — message us and we will check it with you.

Google TV / Android TV

Google TV, Sony, TCL, Hisense, Shield & similar

Set quality & Direct Play

  1. Open the movies & series app, select your profile, then Settings → Video Quality.
  2. Set both Home and Internet / Remote quality to Maximum / Original.
  3. Set Direct Play to Auto or On and keep Direct Stream on.
  4. If you use a soundbar, set Audio Passthrough to HDMI or Auto in the app settings.

If it buffers or transcodes:

  • This is our recommended platform, so it should Direct Play almost everything. If it does not, check that quality did not slip back to a Mbps limit.
  • Restart the TV fully (unplug for 30 seconds) — many TVs only sleep and never really restart.
  • For 4K, a wired network cable is best. On Wi-Fi, stay on 5 GHz and in the same room as the router if you can.

Samsung Smart TV

Samsung TVs that run Tizen

Set quality & Direct Play

  1. Open the movies & series app on the TV and go to Settings → Video.
  2. Set Local Quality and Remote Quality to Original / Maximum and remove any bitrate limit.
  3. Turn on Direct Play and Direct Stream if they appear in the list.
  4. Note: on Samsung, live TV does not run natively — these steps are for movies & series. For live TV, ask us about a small Google TV or Fire TV Stick for this TV.

If it buffers or transcodes:

  • Samsung TVs support fewer audio and subtitle types, so transcoding happens more often here. Try turning subtitles off or choosing a different audio track first.
  • Fully restart the TV: hold the remote power button until the TV turns off and the logo appears again.
  • If the same title always transcodes on Samsung but plays fine elsewhere, that is a TV limit, not your internet — a plug-in streaming device usually fixes it.

LG Smart TV

LG TVs that run webOS

Set quality & Direct Play

  1. Open the movies & series app on the TV and go to Settings → Video Quality.
  2. Set Home and Remote quality to Maximum / Original. Do not leave a Mbps cap on.
  3. Enable Direct Play where shown, and leave quality on Original for local playback.
  4. Note: on LG, live TV does not run natively — these steps are for movies & series. For live TV, ask us about a small streaming device for this TV.

If it buffers or transcodes:

  • Like Samsung, LG supports a narrower set of formats. If a file transcodes, try another audio track or subtitles off to confirm the cause.
  • Restart the TV from Settings → General → System, or unplug it for 30 seconds.
  • Keep the app updated from the LG Content Store — old versions transcode more. If problems continue, a Google TV or Fire TV Stick on this TV is the simplest long-term fix.
